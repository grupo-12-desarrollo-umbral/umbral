# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Primary:** Game admins and operators who create missions, manage live game sessions, and control the real-time competitive experience. They are often under time pressure, managing multiple teams, and need confidence that the system will respond instantly.

**Secondary:** Players and teams who land on the site to discover, join, and understand the game. They arrive curious and competitive, looking for a memorable experience.

**Context:** The interface exists in the heat of live gameplay. Decisions happen in seconds. The tool must feel as responsive and reliable as the game itself.

## Product Purpose

Umbral is the command center and landing surface for live competitive game experiences (trivia challenges, treasure hunts, and clue races). It enables operators to design missions, launch sessions, and steer real-time gameplay while giving players a compelling entry point into the world.

Success looks like: operators managing complex live games without friction, and players feeling the thrill of the competition from the first interaction.

## Brand Personality

Warm, magical, playful.

The interface should feel like a trusted game master: inviting, surprising in the right moments, and always in control. It borrows the tactile charm and emotional warmth of Nintendo, Pokémon, and Animal Crossing, without ever becoming childish or cartoonish. Every interaction should suggest that something delightful might happen, while maintaining the precision and reliability operators need.

Voice: encouraging, concise, and slightly wonder-filled. Never dry, never patronizing.

## Anti-references

- **Overly childish / cartoonish:** Candy-bright palettes, bubbly rounded everything, infantilizing language, or toy-like aesthetics that undermine credibility.
- **Corporate SaaS clichés:** Generic blue-gradient dashboards, big hero metrics, stock illustrations, "trusted by" logo grids, and sterile feature lists.
- **Dark / edgy gamer aesthetic:** Neon-on-black, aggressive typography, esports branding, cyberpunk motifs, or loud visual noise.
- **Cold minimalism:** White-space-only layouts that feel lifeless, uninviting, or devoid of personality.

## Design Principles

1. **Playful precision.** Magical moments and delightful details must be built on top of rock-solid, predictable interactions. Delight never comes at the cost of clarity.
2. **Warm authority.** Operators should feel confident and in control, but the interface should never feel clinical or cold. Authority is expressed through calm competence, not harsh structure.
3. **Alive, not animated.** Motion and transitions should serve the live, real-time nature of the game. Avoid decorative animation; every movement should communicate state, progress, or response.
4. **Approachable depth.** Complex game management (missions, sessions, leaderboards, evidence) should feel simple and inviting on the surface, with depth revealed progressively as needed.
5. **Threshold moments.** Celebrate transitions and progress. "Umbral" means threshold; the design should honor moments of crossing over: joining a game, leveling up, solving a clue, or a session going live.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **WCAG AAA** compliance target where possible, especially for contrast ratios on critical gameplay and operator controls.
- **Reduced motion support:** Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Core functionality must work without animation; motion is enhancement, not requirement.
- **Colorblind-friendly considerations:** Game states and mission types should not rely on color alone. Use shape, pattern, label, or icon redundancy.
- **Keyboard operability:** Admin workflows must be fully keyboard-navigable for speed and inclusivity.
