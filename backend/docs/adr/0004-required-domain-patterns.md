# Required design patterns for bounded-context responsibilities

Umbral adopts a small set of required design patterns in places where the domain would otherwise sprawl into handler conditionals, duplicated validation flows, or ambiguous lifecycle logic. `Strategy` is required for score calculation and mode-specific progression policies, `Composite` for `Mission` and hierarchical `MissionNode` modeling, `Facade` for session orchestration and event publication in `SessionOperations`, `Proxy` for role and policy-based access guards in service and presentation layers, `Template Method` for stable validation workflows with mode-specific steps, `State` for `LiveSession` lifecycle transitions, and `Chain of Responsibility` for composable validation of QR submissions, trivia answers, and session-state changes; these mappings are mandatory guidance for implementation in `docs/ddd_solution_model.md` and the service `CONTEXT.md` files.
